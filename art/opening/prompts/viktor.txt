Picture prompts for a storybook opening (5 pictures). Draw ONE picture per request, in order, only when I ask.
Style for every picture: Hand-painted storybook illustration for a kamishibai (Japanese picture-story) card. Landscape 3:2. Painterly gouache and ink, visible brush texture, warm muted earthy palette, soft dramatic light. Fantasy world mixing Western medieval, Japanese and Chinese elements. Grounded proportions, not chibi, not photoreal. No text, no letters, no border.
Keep the main character's face, hair, age and outfit consistent with the attached portrait (except where a picture explicitly shows them as a child).
Each picture: landscape 1536x1024.
